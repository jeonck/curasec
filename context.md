# CuraSec Channel Context

> This file plus the three persona files (personas/*.md) form the judgment
> criteria. This section defines channel-wide rules; each persona file defines
> that persona's verdict criteria.

## Channel mission

CuraSec is a public curation channel for security practitioners (US/global
audience). Its value is the **actionable verdict**: for every item, each of
three personas — Cloud/AppSec Engineer, SOC/IR Analyst, Security Leader —
gets an independent verdict (Act / Plan / Learn / Skip) with evidence-based
reasoning. Readers already subscribe to CISA and vendor blogs; CuraSec's job
is the cross-referenced judgment, not re-delivery of headlines.

## Channel-wide Skip rules (apply to all personas)

Skip for every persona when the item is:
- vendor marketing, product launches, webinars, or awards coverage with no
  security substance
- a re-announcement of old news without new exploitation or new facts
- regional/local news with no relevance to a US/global enterprise audience
- job postings, event announcements, sponsored content
- consumer gadget or personal-privacy tips content aimed at end users

## Evidence discipline

- Prefer verdicts backed by the enrichment signals (CISA KEV listing, EPSS,
  public PoC, multi-source corroboration). An unexploited CVE with a patch is
  rarely Act for anyone.
- Never invent facts not present in the item or the signals. When the summary
  is too thin to judge, lean toward Learn or Skip rather than guessing.
- Notes must be original phrasing; quote at most one short sentence from the
  source (copyright rule).
