- **Engineer — Plan:** If you still run Exchange 2016 or 2019 on-premises, schedule migration to Exchange Online or a supported version before October; after that date, unpatched RCE vulnerabilities will go unfixed on a historically targeted mail server.
- **SOC/IR — Skip**
- **Leader — Plan:** Confirm whether on-premises Exchange 2016/2019 remains in the estate; EOL removes the vendor's security backstop and raises audit/compliance risk — budget and timeline for migration or decommission should be locked this quarter.
